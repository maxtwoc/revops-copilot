# RevOps Copilot

An AI copilot for revenue teams. It turns messy CRM exports into a pipeline
view people trust, and answers questions about data and the sales playbook with citations.

## The problem
Growing sales teams run on CRM data full of duplicates, missing stages and
stale deals. Leaders don't trust the pipeline numbers, analysts lose days
cleaning spreadsheets before every forecast, and reps can't find answers in
the sales playbook when they need them.

Inspired by client work where leadership teams couldn't agree on whose
numbers to trust.

## What it will do
- **Clean:** load CRM exports and flag duplicates, missing fields and stale deals
- **See:** show the funnel: conversion by stage, win rate, pipeline coverage
- **Ask:** answer questions like "which deals over £50k are stalled?" in plain English
- **Trust:** cite a source for every answer and log every query for audit
